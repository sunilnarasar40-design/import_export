About Us

India's Finest Produce,
Delivered Worldwide

Sunil C. Narasar Import Export is a dedicated agri-export business based in India, specialising in the export of premium fresh vegetables and rice to the UAE and the United States of America.

Our operations are built on a foundation of trust, quality, and timely delivery. We work directly with farmers across leading agricultural belts in India to source the freshest produce and ensure it reaches international markets in perfect condition.

From careful post-harvest handling and hygienic packaging to seamless export documentation and refrigerated logistics — every step reflects our commitment to global standards.
